Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains cancellations and refunds for NovoX Media subscriptions paid via Razorpay.

1. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription anytime from Plans & Billing in your account. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for; it will not renew after that.

2. Refund eligibility

  • New subscriptions: if you are not satisfied, you may request a refund within 7 days of the first payment for that plan, provided the plan's premium features have not been substantially used.
  • Renewals: charges for an auto-renewed period are generally non-refundable once the new period has begun, but contact us — we review genuine cases (e.g. accidental renewal reported promptly).
  • Downgrades/upgrades take effect from the next cycle; we do not pro-rate part-used periods unless required by law.

3. Non-refundable items

  • Money spent on third-party platforms (e.g. Meta/Google ad spend or WhatsApp conversation charges).
  • Add-on or prepaid credits that have been consumed.
  • Periods already used before a cancellation request.

4. How to request a refund

Email medianovox@gmail.com from your registered email with your account details and reason. We may ask for basic information to verify the request.

5. Processing time

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days (your bank may take longer to reflect it).

6. Contact

Need help? Email medianovox@gmail.com.
