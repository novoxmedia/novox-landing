Digital Delivery & Fulfillment Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NovoX Media is a fully digital, software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform. There are no physical goods, no shipping, and no physical delivery involved. This policy describes how access to our platform and features is granted to subscribers.

1. Nature of the product

NovoX Media provides online access to an AI-powered social media marketing platform. All features — including AI content generation, post scheduling, campaign management, WhatsApp marketing tools, analytics and the AI assistant — are delivered entirely via our web application at app.novoxmedia.in.

2. Instant digital delivery

Upon successful completion of payment via Razorpay, your subscription is activated immediately and automatically. No manual steps are required on our end. You will:

  • Receive a payment confirmation email from Razorpay to your registered email address.
  • See your plan status updated to "Active" in your NovoX Media account dashboard within minutes.
  • Have instant access to all features included in your chosen plan (Deluxe, Pro or Elite).

3. Access method

  • Access is provided through your registered account at our web application.
  • You can log in from any modern web browser — no app download or installation required.
  • Access continues for the full paid period (monthly or yearly) from the date of payment.

4. Payment failure or pending payments

If a payment is pending or fails (e.g. bank decline, session timeout), your plan will not be activated. Razorpay may hold a temporary authorisation on your account which is automatically released within 5–7 business days. No charge is applied for failed payments. If you believe a payment was deducted but your plan was not activated, email us at medianovox@gmail.com with your transaction ID and we will resolve it within 24 business hours.

5. Plan renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle. Access to the platform continues seamlessly. You will receive a renewal reminder email before the next charge. You can cancel auto-renewal anytime from your account dashboard under Plans & Billing.

6. Downtime and service availability

We aim for 99.9% uptime. Scheduled maintenance is communicated in advance where possible. In the event of unexpected downtime exceeding 24 consecutive hours, affected users may be eligible for a service credit — contact us to apply.

7. No physical shipping

NovoX Media does not sell or ship any physical products. There are no shipping charges, tracking numbers, or courier services involved in any transaction. All goods and services are delivered digitally via our platform.

8. Contact

For any questions about your subscription activation or access: medianovox@gmail.com · +91 87674 70456
