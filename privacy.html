Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NovoX Media ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use our website and AI social‑media marketing platform (the "Service"), how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details you provide: name, email, phone, company and password (stored hashed).
  • Marketing content you create or upload: posts, campaigns, banners, brand details and leads.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment processor Razorpay. We do not store your full card / UPI details on our servers — only a payment reference and status.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, and basic device/log information to keep the Service secure and reliable.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate and improve the Service and your account.
  • To generate content, run/simulate campaigns, and deliver the features in your plan.
  • To process subscriptions and payments, and send service-related notices.
  • To provide support and to detect, prevent and address fraud or abuse.

3. Third-party services we use

We share the minimum data needed with trusted providers: Razorpay (payments), Meta / WhatsApp Business and Google (when you connect those channels to publish or advertise), Anthropic (AI generation), and our cloud database/hosting providers. Each processes data under its own terms.

4. Data security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS), hashed passwords and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

5. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent for optional processing. To exercise these rights, email us at medianovox@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies / local storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences. We do not sell your personal data.

8. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email medianovox@gmail.com.
